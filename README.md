# 𖦹 THE ANSWERS

**"The Answers"** is a minimalist, digital interpretation of traditional oracle decks. Moving beyond a simple "Yes/No" application, this project focuses on the intersection of synchronicity, intuition, and the non-linear nature of clarity.

## ✨ Philosophy
Inspired by the beauty of **Chaos Theory** and the **Butterfly Effect**, this deck suggests that the answer we seek is rarely a binary 'yes' or 'no'. Sometimes, the most profound answer is a shift in perspective. 

The 33 cards within this deck are curated to provide either direct confirmation or a philosophical nudge—inviting the user to "See the Bigger Picture" and trust the timing of their personal evolution.

## 🛠️ Technical Overview
- **Vanilla Tech Stack:** Built purely with HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Mathematical Fan Algorithm:** Cards are dynamically arranged along a precise arc using trigonometric principles for a natural "deck" feel.
- **Responsive Architecture:** Utilizes `clamp()` and viewport units (`vh/vw`) to ensure a seamless experience across iPhones, iPads, and desktops.
- **Interactive UI:** A user-driven interaction model where the user selects, flips, and dismisses cards manually to enhance the sense of agency.

## 🎨 Aesthetic Design
- **Color Palette:** A deep, royal Burgundy (`#5b0e2d`) set against a dark cosmic background.
- **Symbolism:** Featuring the **𖦹 (Spiral)**, representing wisdom, growth, and the cyclical nature of the universe.
- **Typography:** A sophisticated contrast between the elegant *Cormorant Garamond* for guidance and modern sans-serif for clarity.
